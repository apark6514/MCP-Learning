Fluffy Pancakes (serves 4)

Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 3 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 1/4 cups milk
- 1 egg
- 3 tbsp melted butter

Steps:
1. Whisk the dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
2. Make a well in the center; pour in milk, egg, and butter. Mix until smooth.
3. Heat a lightly oiled griddle over medium-high heat.
4. Pour 1/4 cup batter per pancake. Flip when bubbles form. Cook until golden.
